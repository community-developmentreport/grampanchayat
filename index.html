Agriculture & Farmers

Supporting paddy, arecanut & coconut cultivators with credit, insurance & market access

01 / 10

PM-KISAN

Central Scheme
ObjectiveAssured income support to farmers
EligibilityAll landholding farmer families (excl. IT payers)
Benefits
  • ₹6,000/year in 3 instalments of ₹2,000 via DBT
  • Mandatory eKYC required
Apply Viapmkisan.gov.in or nearest CSC
02 / 10

Kisan Credit Card (KCC)

Central Scheme
ObjectiveTimely & affordable credit for farmers
EligibilityFarmers in crop cultivation, allied activities & fisheries
Benefits
  • Revolving cash credit facility
  • ~7% p.a. interest; 4% with subvention
Apply ViaApply at SBI, HDFC or NABARD-linked cooperative banks
03 / 10

Soil Health Card Scheme

Central Scheme
ObjectivePromote balanced fertilizer use
EligibilityAll farmers
Benefits
  • Free soil testing & nutrient analysis
  • 25 crore+ cards distributed
Apply Viasoilhealth.dac.gov.in or local Agriculture dept.
04 / 10

Apply ViaState Level Sanctioning Committees
05 / 10

NMSA

Central Scheme
ObjectiveClimate-resilient farming practices
EligibilityFarmers in rainfed & climate-vulnerable areas
Benefits

Since2014–15

PMFBY – Fasal Bima Yojana

Central Scheme
ObjectiveAffordable crop insurance
EligibilityAll farmers (voluntary since Kharif 2020)
Benefits
  • 2% Kharif · 1.5% Rabi · 5% Horticulture premium

Apply Viapmfby.gov.in or via banks & CSCs

Portalenam.gov.in
