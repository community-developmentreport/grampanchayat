About This Report

This report consolidates demographic data, community facilities, available resources, and over 60 government schemes applicable to residents of Nitte Grampanchayat. It aims to support transparent planning based on real community needs and ensure no eligible household is left unaware of the benefits available to them.

Agriculture & Farmers

Supporting paddy, arecanut & coconut cultivators with credit, insurance & market access

✦ ✦ ✦
01 / 10

PM-KISAN

Central Scheme
ObjectiveAssured income support to farmers
EligibilityAll landholding farmer families (excluding IT payers)
Benefits
  • ₹6,000/year in 3 instalments of ₹2,000 via DBT
  • Mandatory eKYC required
How to ApplyPM-KISAN portal or CSCs
02 / 10

Kisan Credit Card (KCC)

Central Scheme
ObjectiveTimely & affordable credit for farmers
EligibilityFarmers in crop cultivation, allied activities & fisheries
Benefits
  • Revolving cash credit facility
  • ~7% p.a. interest; 4% with subvention
How to ApplyVia SBI or NABARD-linked banks
03 / 10

Soil Health Card Scheme

Central Scheme
ObjectivePromote balanced fertilizer use
EligibilityAll farmers
Benefits
  • Free soil testing & nutrient analysis
  • Fertilizer & organic input recommendations
  • 25 crore+ cards distributed
How to ApplySHC portal / Agriculture dept.
04 / 10

RKVY – Raftaar

Central Scheme
ObjectiveAccelerate agricultural growth via innovation
EligibilityState govts propose; farmers benefit indirectly
Benefits
  • Grants for modernisation & agri-startups
  • Food security & sustainable farming
How to ApplyVia State Level Sanctioning Committees
05 / 10

NMSA

Central Scheme
ObjectiveClimate-resilient farming practices
EligibilityFarmers in rainfed & climate-vulnerable areas
Benefits
  • Integrated farming systems
  • Drip/sprinkler irrigation support
  • Soil & resource conservation
Since2014–15
06 / 10

PMFBY – Fasal Bima Yojana

Central Scheme
ObjectiveAffordable crop insurance
EligibilityAll farmers (voluntary since Kharif 2020)
Benefits
  • 2% premium (Kharif), 1.5% (Rabi), 5% (Horticulture)
  • Covers drought, flood, pests & post-harvest loss
How to ApplyBanks, CSCs or insurance companies
07 / 10

e-NAM

Central Scheme
ObjectiveUnified national market for agri commodities
EligibilityFarmers registered with APMC mandis
Benefits
  • Online transparent price discovery
  • 1,400+ mandis integrated
  • Eliminates middlemen
Since2016, via SFAC
08 / 10

PKVY – Organic Farming

Central Scheme
ObjectivePromote organic farming & soil health
EligibilityFarmers organised into clusters
Benefits
  • Financial support for organic inputs & certification
  • 15 lakh ha under organic farming
  • 25 lakh farmers benefitted
PortalJaivik Kheti portal
09 / 10

Krishi Bhagya Karnataka

ObjectiveWater conservation for dryland farmers
EligibilityFarmers in rainfed areas of Karnataka

Raitha Siri Karnataka
